# PocketScan 📱✨

**PocketScan** is a powerful, lightweight, and offline-first productivity app designed to make document handling and digital management effortless on your mobile device. Built for speed, privacy, and everyday utility.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **📄 Smart Document Scanner:** Quickly scan, crop, and digitize papers, ID cards, and official documents with crystal-clear enhancement quality.
* **📸 Instant Passport Photo Maker:** Need official photos urgently? Resize, crop, and format your pictures instantly into standard passport dimensions right from your phone, saving studio visits!
* **🔍 QR & Barcode Scanner:** Instant, lightweight code reading capabilities built right into your daily workflow.
* **⚡ Professional File Tools:** Effortlessly compress heavy images, merge multiple PDFs, and convert formats on the go.
* **🔒 Secure Vault:** Keep your private documents and confidential photos safely locked behind an encrypted passcode.
* **💯 100% Offline & Private:** Your data stays securely on your device. No unwanted cloud uploads, ensuring maximum privacy.
